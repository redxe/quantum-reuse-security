\section*{Acknowledgments and AI Contribution Statement}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Acknowledgments and AI Contribution Statement}

The initial circuit exploration was inspired by a copy of
\emph{Programming Quantum Computers} given to Vi Connelly by her mother. The
author also acknowledges the open educational value of Quirk and the book's
maintained examples and errata. The compact navigation, modular sectioning, and
end-to-end emphasis of this revision were inspired by the survey presentation
of Dalzell et al.~\cite{dalzell2024survey}.

GPT-5.6 Thinking (OpenAI) contributed substantial assistance in mathematical
exposition, branch-conditioned analysis, source-code generation, LaTeX
implementation, numerical consistency checks, figure design, reference
auditing, repository review, and manuscript revision. Vi Connelly supplied the
original observations, circuit exports, research questions, interpretive
corrections, semantic wire model, and motivating security hypothesis. GitHub
Copilot was used as a coding agent during repository restructuring, test and CI
configuration, and patch application. The author reviewed the resulting changes,
and GitHub Actions was used to verify tests and deterministic outputs.

The purpose of crediting AI contributions prominently is not to imply that an AI
system can accept responsibility for a scientific publication. It cannot. The
purpose is to make the production process visible. The human author retains
responsibility for the final claims and for any future submission, while the
included code and data allow readers to inspect the parts of the argument that
were developed with computational assistance.
